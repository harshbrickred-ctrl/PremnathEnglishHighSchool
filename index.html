About the school

On Station Road since 2013.

Boys and girls study together in English, from the first classroom through the primary years.

The campus is at Survey No. 215, near Hasova Mandir, Juhu Chandra, Naigaon East, Palghar 401208.

Families can call or visit the office until 6:00 pm to ask about a class, a section, or admissions.

Academics

Three stages, one campus.

Choose a stage to read about it. The office confirms the current section and age group when you call.

01

Nursery

A first classroom for the youngest children: routines, play, and the start of spoken English.

02

Kindergarten

Stories, numbers, and more time speaking and listening in English.

03

Primary

Reading, writing, and the subjects of the school day, still taught in English.
